# Brag Plan: Dark Pattern Detector

## What is this app?
Dark Pattern Detector is an AI-powered visual forensic tool that analyzes screenshots of websites and mobile apps to unmask deceptive, manipulative design tricks—translating sneaky countdowns, hidden subscriptions, and guilt-trip buttons into plain-English reality checks with ethical redesign alternatives.

## The angle
A story-led detective journey: moving from the everyday frustration of feeling rushed and tricked at checkout, to taking control by dropping the specimen into Dark Pattern Detector, having AI forensics unmask the hidden manipulation, and revealing the ethical fix.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An authentic mobile checkout interface with a high-pressure flashing red urgency banner: "Only 2 left in stock — Sale ends in 02:14:33". A bold question strikes: "Ever feel like a checkout button is rushing you on purpose?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Forensic Snapping Tag**: Crimson bounding box and handwritten scribble arrow pinpointing the fake countdown timer with plain-English translation.
- **The Drop & Scan**: Organic arc drag-and-drop into the paper dropzone, triggering an AI forensic radar sweep with live status analysis.
- **The 3-Pattern Autopsy & Deception Odometer**: Sequential arrival of unmasked patterns (False Urgency, Preselection, Confirmshaming) with the deception score odometer climbing to 88.
- **The Ethical Fix Reveal**: Toggle switch that transforms the manipulative specimen into an honest, ethical design alternative.

## Outro / punchline
"Scan before you pay. Expose the traps." Brand lockup with detective mascot, "DARK PATTERN DETECTOR" wordmark, and clean URL `darkpatterns.dev`.

## User flow worth showing
Encounter shady checkout → snap & drop into Dark Pattern Detector → AI forensic sweep unmasks 3 deceptive patterns → review plain-English autopsy & ethical fix → checkout with confidence.

- Creative direction: Story-led forensic unmasking of deceptive e-commerce tricks
