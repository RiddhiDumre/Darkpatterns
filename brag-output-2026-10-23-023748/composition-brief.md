## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Story-led forensic unmasking of deceptive e-commerce tricks
- Interpretation: Crisp, high-contrast visual precision with snappy tactile motion, editorial craft paper aesthetic, and rhythmic beat alignment that keeps tension and payoff high without feeling chaotic.
- Angle: A relatable shopping experience where manufactured urgency triggers an instant forensic autopsy, uncovering real dark patterns and proving that ethical design is possible.
- Hook: 3D phone specimen showing the ticking fake countdown with a bold question: "Ever get the feeling a checkout button is rushing you on purpose?"
- Outro: Brand wordmark with detective mascot and clear call to action: "Scan before you pay. Expose the traps. darkpatterns.dev"
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS buzzwords ("supercharge your workflow", "AI-powered synergy")
  - Abstract filler or disconnected stock footage
  - Text disappearing before viewers have settled reading time
